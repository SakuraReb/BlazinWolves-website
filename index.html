About Sakura

The Alpha behind the pack.

Sakura Noctis Vale is an anime-style author and songwriter, and the Alpha of BlazinWolves. Her songs pour out daily; her stories run dark, hungry, and loyal to the bone.

Her wolf is Blazi — a large white wolf with pink highlights and pink eyes, a cherry-blossom mark on her forehead. Girl and wolf are one and the same: same blood, different beast.

She writes the Blood Moon saga and the BlazinWolves Chronicles, and she leads the way she writes — head high, pack first. Bowing is something other people do: she is an Alpha who kneels to no one, and her wolves rise because she never asks them to kneel either.
